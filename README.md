# proof-runner
Proof Runner 🎮
A retro Mario-style pixel platformer set inside the Cysic network.

Play as Annie, a CyRunner on a mission to save the network from The Null Prover, a villain who fakes proofs to take control. Run, jump and smash bricks with your head across 10 stages, from sunny grasslands and mining caves to the Venus Labs and the final core battle.

Power-ups
🟦 DogeBox1 makes Annie bigger
🔫 VENUS gun lets her shoot
⭐ CYOPS star makes her invincible for a few seconds
⚡ ZK Air for speed, ZK Pro for higher jumps, and a Cube for double score

Secrets to find
Hidden C1 chips, bonus pipe rooms, a secret CyCade arcade and 4 ComputeFi orbs that open the final gate.

Controls
Arrow keys, or A/D + Space to jump. Ctrl to fire, Down to enter pipes. Touch buttons work on mobile.

Beat the boss and see what's waiting for you: Welcome To The Future of ComputeFi 🎆
